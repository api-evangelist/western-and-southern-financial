# Western & Southern Financial (western-and-southern-financial)

Western & Southern Financial Group is a diversified family of financial services companies headquartered in Cincinnati, Ohio, offering life insurance, annuities, mutual funds, asset management, and other financial products through multiple subsidiary brands including Western & Southern Life, Gerber Life, Columbus Life, and Fort Washington Investment Advisors.
